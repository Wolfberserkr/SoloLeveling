(Daily Training Quest, Gym Dungeons, Fuel Protocol). The System does not
promise magic. It promises arithmetic, applied daily.

## The arithmetic

- **Maintenance (TDEE)** ≈ BMR 1 967 × 1.4–1.5 activity ≈ **2 800–2 950 kcal/day** once training daily.
- **Intake target: 2 100–2 200 kcal/day** → ≈ 700 kcal daily deficit ≈ **0.7 kg of fat per week**, a safe-aggressive rate at this starting weight.
- **Protein: 182 g/day** (the calculator's optimal; range 131–197 g). That is
  ≈ 2.4 g per kg of fat-free mass — the level shown to preserve muscle in a
  deficit. This is the Fuel Protocol's default target in the app.
- **Supplement stack (daily, checked off in the Fuel panel):**
  creatine monohydrate 5 g (any time, no loading) · vitamin D3 + omega-3 with
  a meal. Whey protein counts toward the 182 g and is the cheapest way to hit it.
- **Sleep 8 h** — it is literally the mana economy, and in a deficit it is
  the difference between losing fat and losing muscle.

**Six-month projection at that rate: −18 to −24 kg → ≈ 93–98 kg, ~25–27 %
body fat.** Honest note: the full "Spartan" look (visible abs, ~15 % at
~87 kg) is a 10–12-month boss. Month 6 is the midpoint raid: visibly lean,
conditioned, strong — undeniably *in shape*.

## The campaign, mapped to the System

Four gym sessions a week (the Upper/Lower dungeon cycle), the Daily Training
Quest every day, protein logged in the Fuel panel, metrics logged weekly.

| Months | Dungeon | Runs | Focus | Exit boss (benchmarks) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 – 1.5 | **Phase 1 — Foundation** | 24 | Learn movements, build the habit, joint-friendly volume | The Gatekeeper: 20 push-ups · 60 s plank · 30 squats · 2 km run |
| 2 – 3 | **Phase 2 — Iron Path** | 24 | Barbell linear progression, add weight every session | Iron Golem: squat 0.75×BW×5 · bench 0.5×BW×5 · deadlift 1×BW×3 · 1 pull-up |
| 3.5 – 5 | **Phase 3 — Warrior's Forge** | 30 | Volume + engine work | Berserker Knight: squat 1×BW×5 · bench 0.75×BW×5 · deadlift 1.25×BW×3 · 5 pull-ups · 5 km run |

A quiet double win the System already accounts for: boss benchmarks scale by
**bodyweight**. Every kilogram of fat dropped makes the squat multiple, the
pull-up, and the 5 km cheaper at the same absolute strength.

## Standing orders

1. **Protect the joints early.** At BMI 36 the run targets are best served as
   brisk incline walking, bike, or walk-run intervals for the first 6–8
   weeks — log the distance honestly, the System counts kilometres, not ego.
   Use each exercise's demo link in the dungeon before loading it.
2. **Never miss twice.** The System already softens a failed day (−15 % load,
   streak shield skill later). One missed day is recovery; two is a pattern.
3. **Log weight once a week, same morning, after waking.** The app keeps one
   refined entry per week — the trend line is the truth, single days are noise.
4. **Eat the protein first.** 182 g ÷ 4 meals ≈ 45 g each. Hit protein and
   the calorie ceiling mostly takes care of itself.
5. **Recovery days are quests, not failures.** Poor sleep or low mana
   retargets the day on purpose. Complete it; take the potion.
6. Weight stalls for 2+ weeks → the weekly System Coach will deload or nudge
   the load; trust it, and re-check the calorie count before cutting further.

*This is a plan, not medical advice — at this starting BMI a check-in with a
doctor before month 1, and again around month 3, is the professional move.*
